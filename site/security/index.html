Security reporting

Report suspected security issues to support@pawkeepr.cc with the subject “Security report”.

Describe the affected feature, device or browser, approximate time and steps needed to reproduce the problem. Use a test account and remove personal information from screenshots.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys or other users’ records. Do not access or change records belonging to anyone else.

For ordinary account help, visit Support.

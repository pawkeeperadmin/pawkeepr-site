Support

For help with pawkeepr, email support@pawkeepr.cc.

Include useful details

Describe what happened, the app version, your device model and iOS or Android version. Include an approximate time and any error message. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, payment card details or private keys.

Account and records

Use Settings in the app to export your data or request account deletion. If you cannot sign in, contact support from the email address associated with your account. See account deletion guidance.

Privacy and security

Read our privacy information and terms. For a security concern, follow these reporting instructions.

pawkeepr helps organize pet care records. For urgent animal health concerns, contact your veterinarian.

Privacy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

pawkeepr is private by default. We collect only the information required to run the app and support your account.

Data we process

  • Account details (for sign-in and account management)
  • Pet records, reminders, and care entries you create
  • Attachments and documents you upload
  • Operational diagnostics and analytics for reliability

How data is used

  • Deliver app features and sync your records
  • Send notifications you have enabled
  • Improve performance and stability

Your controls

  • AI features can be turned on or off in-app
  • You can export your data
  • You can request account deletion

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with required service providers, user-initiated actions, or legal requirements.

For privacy, legal, or security requests, contact support@pawkeepr.cc.

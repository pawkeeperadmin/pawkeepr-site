About pawkeepr

pawkeepr is built to make pet care feel calmer, clearer, and easier to keep up with.

Instead of scattered notes, photos, and reminders, pawkeepr gives families one place to manage care events, caregivers, timelines, and documents.

What matters most

  • Simple day-to-day logging
  • Reliable reminders and follow-through
  • Organized care history across pets
  • Optional AI features under user control

Product direction

The website helps you discover pawkeepr. The app is where care happens.
